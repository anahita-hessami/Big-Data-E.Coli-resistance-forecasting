# Source update notes — 1 September 2026

## EARS-Net

ECDC published the 2024 EARS-Net annual report on 18 November 2025. A live rerun of the exact Surveillance Atlas measure endpoint used by this project, with the requested interval extended through 2024, still returned a maximum year of 2023: 3,192 country–year–antibiotic rows across 30 countries. The scientific input therefore remains through 2023 until the matching machine-readable resistant and tested counts can be retrieved. Report-level percentages are not mixed with the existing count extract.

Official report: https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/publications-data/antimicrobial-resistance-eueea-ears-net-annual-epidemiological-report-2024

## ESAC-Net

ECDC published the 2024 consumption report on 18 November 2025. The project requires a complete machine-readable historical dashboard export because the current dashboard applies the latest ATC/DDD index retrospectively. New 2023–2024 values must not simply be appended to the older 2022 workbook. The normalized full-history export has not yet been obtained, so consumption remains through 2022 and absent prescribing variables remain missing rather than being fabricated.

Official report: https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/publications-data/antimicrobial-consumption-eueea-esac-net-annual-epidemiological-report-2024

Dashboard: https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/antimicrobial-consumption/surveillance-and-disease-data/database

## Demographics

World Bank indicators were refreshed through 2024. The input now contains 750 country-year rows. Population, age 65+, urbanization and GDP per capita are available for all 30 countries in 2024; health expenditure remains incomplete for that year. Because AMR outcomes currently end in 2023, the extra demographic year does not enter current labelled model rows but is ready for later forecast origins.
